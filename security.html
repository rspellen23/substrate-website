Security

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This page describes how Substrate protects the source documents and course content you process through the service. It complements our Privacy Policy, which covers what we collect and how it's used.

Data in transit & at rest

Substrate runs locally on your own machine. Your source documents and generated course content are stored and processed there (not on Substrate-operated servers), so there is no Substrate-controlled copy to encrypt at rest; protecting it at rest is a function of your own device's disk encryption and access controls. The only data that leaves your machine is the text you explicitly submit for AI-assisted generation, sent over HTTPS directly to the AI provider you've connected (for example, Anthropic's Claude or OpenAI's ChatGPT, depending on the subscription you use).

Infrastructure & hosting

Substrate does not host or store your source documents or generated course content on our infrastructure. The application runs locally; its dashboard is served by a small local web server bound only to your own computer (127.0.0.1), not reachable from the internet or any other device. Our marketing website (substrate.io) is hosted separately and never receives or stores your course content.

Access controls

Because your source documents and generated course content are never transmitted to or stored on Substrate-operated systems, Substrate personnel do not have access to them. Access to our marketing website and support tooling, which never receive your course content, follows standard least-privilege practices.

Third-party sub-processors

Substrate itself does not process your source documents or generated course content as a sub-processor, since that content stays on your machine. If you use AI-assisted content generation, the text you submit for that purpose is sent directly to the AI provider you've connected under your own subscription (for example, Anthropic or OpenAI); that provider's own data-handling terms govern that processing, not a data-processing agreement between you and Substrate. We recommend reviewing your chosen AI provider's own privacy and data-use policies.

Data retention & deletion

Since Substrate does not store your source documents or generated course content, retention and deletion are entirely under your control on your own machine: uninstalling Substrate or deleting your local project files removes it completely. We hold nothing of yours to delete on our end.

Compliance

Substrate does not currently hold a SOC 2, ISO 27001, or other third-party security certification.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in Substrate, please report it to hello@substrate.io. We ask that you give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address a report before any public disclosure.
