Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Substrate ("we," "us") handles information in connection with the Substrate service.

Information we collect

When you request a demo or contact us, we collect the information you provide directly (such as your email address). Substrate itself runs locally on your machine: the source documents you open in the app and the courses generated from them are processed there to provide the service; they are not uploaded to or stored on Substrate-operated servers.

How we use information

  • To respond to demo requests and support inquiries
  • To provide, maintain, and improve the service
  • To communicate with you about your account or the service

Data storage & security

Substrate does not store your source documents or generated course content: that content stays on your machine, where its retention and deletion are entirely under your control. We apply industry-standard safeguards to the account and contact information we do collect (above): encrypted connections, access controls, and regular security review. We do not sell your data or your learners' data to third parties. See our Security page for more detail on how your course content is protected by never leaving your machine.

Third-party services

We use a limited set of third-party providers to operate Substrate's website and account/support communications. If you use AI-assisted content generation, the text you submit for that purpose is sent directly to the AI provider you've connected under your own subscription (for example, Anthropic or OpenAI), and is not routed through or stored by Substrate. We do not use your content to train third-party AI models beyond what is necessary to generate your requested output, and we recommend reviewing your chosen AI provider's own privacy practices.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California). To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time, consistent with applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated date on this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@substrate.io.
